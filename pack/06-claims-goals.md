## Purpose

Separate what AltGrep may claim from what it will not. **Highest canon** in this pack.

## Facts

- Australian secondary sources: you commercialise rights around an idea, you do not sell a raw idea as property. [web:94]
- USPTO-style Section 8 cancellation of a mark does not clear a name worldwide (from prior pack).
- Named canon pack/code under `(local paths)`; live on this box under `(local paths)`.
- UI must never shorten “control-proven handle” to “verified.” Decided: no.

## Thesis

Thesis. Over-claiming “safe private marketplace for ideas” creates ACL and fraud risk. Under-claiming “classifieds for odd inventory plus named vouches” is accurate.

### Claims we may make (if true)

- You can list a file, a right, a handle you control, or a block of time.
- Profiles use `@name`.
- Other-service handles require a control proof before they list.
- Vouches are named and revocable.
- CLI and HTTP expose the same verbs (when a verb ships).
- Rooms are invite or inquiry scoped. No public square.

### Goals

- A searchable catalog of unusual listings (seed: handle, consulting, dossier).
- A vouch graph that a stranger can read.
- Deal rooms attached to listings.
- Operator-grade CLI.
- A public prohibited list and an IP notice mailbox (counsel gate).

### Non-goals

- Government Digital ID, KYC product, or AGDIS participation.
- Selling “raw ideas” as guaranteed unique inventions.
- Registering third-party platform accounts for users.
- Escrow or payments / take-rate in P0–P1 (not before P2 + counsel).
- End-to-end encrypted marketing before the crypto is real.
- Social feed, followers, leaderboards.
- Commodity goods (phones, sofas, cars).
- Weapons, drugs, stolen data, exploit kits, doxxing.
- Soulbound identity. Accounts may later transfer by a dedicated flow.
- Replacing lawyers for assignment of trade marks or patents.
- Sibling repos or sibling docs trees.
- GitHub push / release / domain buy / mark filing / paid tier unless Adam explicitly orders that gate.

### Claims we will not make

- “Safer than banks.”
- “Untraceable groups.”
- “We verify legal identity.”
- “AltBay / next eBay” / Altered / bare Alt as this product.
- Earnings promises on dossiers.
- UI “verified” as a substitute for control-proven handle.

### AU / offer (general information only)

Commercialise a right, a file, or a service. Do not sell a thought. Flag Australian Consumer Law risk on offer copy. Do not draft as counsel. For terms, IP notice mailbox, assignment templates, privacy: stop and name counsel as the gate.
