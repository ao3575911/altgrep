## Purpose

Operating thesis for AltGrep as a marketplace plus private infrastructure, not as a social network.

## Facts

- Australian explainers state you generally cannot sell a raw idea; you commercialise surrounding rights (trade mark, copyright, patent, design, confidential information, systems). [web:94] [web:97]
- Online marketplaces commonly run brand-registry / notice programs for registered IP. AltGrep must have a notice channel if it hosts listings. [web:96]
- Incumbents: Gumtree and Facebook Marketplace for local goods; eBay for auctions; Upwork for labour; LinkedIn for reputation theatre; Discord for groups; ENS/Unstoppable for on-chain names. None combine unusual-inventory classifieds with a vouch graph and a CLI.
- Alt (onlyalt.com) already occupies “Alt” in alternative assets / cards. [web:86]
- No escrow or take-rate before P2 and a counsel pass. No invented revenue, user counts, or fee tables.

## Thesis

Thesis. The scarce thing is not another chat app. It is a venue that treats dossiers, rights, handles, and advisory time as first-class listings, and that makes reputation a graph of named vouches instead of a star rating anyone can buy.

### Offer

- Free: profile, search, inquiry thread, receive vouches.
- Paid later (Estimate, not a quote; counsel gate first): featured listing, extra group seats, control-proven-handle challenge processing. Never shorten to UI “verified.”
- Take-rate only if first-party checkout exists after P2 + counsel. v1 can be introduction-only.

### Who pays

- Sellers of unusual inventory who cannot describe the thing on Gumtree without looking like a scam.
- Consultants who want a public @name and a trail of vouches, not a LinkedIn wall.
- Small groups that need a private room tied to a listing (deal room), not a Discord server.

### Wedge

CLI + web parity for operators who already live in terminals, plus a category set Gumtree will not host honestly. Seed listings only: handle, consulting, dossier.

### 24-month sketch (Estimate)

Assumptions: AU-first, introduction marketplace, no escrow, one operator plus one contractor.

- Year 1 revenue: listing boosts only, if any. Treat as near-zero until a paid tier is cleared by counsel.
- Year 1 cost: domain, hosting, counsel, part-time moderation. Estimate. Low five figures AUD if kept deliberately thin.
- Year 2: take-rate or subscriptions only after repeat use is visible and counsel clears it.

Do not treat this sketch as a forecast. Do not invent user counts or fee tables.

### Risks and controls

- Idea-market as a scam magnet. Control: dossier listings are files with copyright claims, not “this patent will issue.” No earnings claims on dossiers.
- Handle listings of other people’s accounts. Control: control-challenge before publish; fail closed.
- Private groups used for unlawful trade. Control: published prohibited list, report path, no “we cannot see anything” marketing if the server can see it.
- ACL / misleading claims. Control: listing templates that forbid earnings and uniqueness guarantees; flag ACL risk; counsel gate on terms.
- eBay / Alt brand confusion. Control: do not use AltBay; do not use bare Alt; do not use Altered.

### Professional stack

Lawyer (marketplace terms, IP notices, privacy), accountant (entity), one backend/frontend contractor. Operator does CLI and information architecture. Do not draft as counsel.

## Actions

1. Entity and terms before paid listings (counsel gate).
2. Decide introduction-only vs escrow before marketing “safe deals.” No escrow before P2 + counsel.
3. Keep inventory unusual. If the catalog fills with phones and sofas, the wedge is dead.
4. No GitHub push unless Adam orders that gate.

## Open questions

- AU company vs sole trader for v1.
- Whether to touch crypto settlement at all.
